Experimental research project

quine

Draw your app or game on a sheet of paper, take a photo, and quine turns your sketch into a real, running app on your phone. Then keep refining it by chatting with the built-in assistant. No coding.

How it works

  1. Sketch the app or game you want on paper.
  2. Take a photo of your sketch.
  3. quine turns it into a real, running app on your phone.
  4. Refine it by chatting with the built-in assistant.

A research experiment

quine is an experimental research project exploring how a phone can become a live, self-modifying programming environment you shape with sketches and plain language. It is coming to Google Play as an experimental research project. Expect rough edges.

Your data

Your worlds and conversations stay on your device. quine has no backend server, no account, and no analytics. You bring your own AI key. See the privacy policy for the details.
