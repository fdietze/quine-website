Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-15

quine is an experimental research app for Android. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it. It is written to be plain and honest, not to be exhaustive legalese.

No backend, no account

quine has no server operated by its developer. There is no account and no login. The "worlds" you create and your conversations are stored locally on your device, in the app's private files. Your content is not uploaded to any server run by the quine developer.

Your OpenRouter API key

quine works with an AI service through OpenRouter. You provide your own OpenRouter API key, entered in the app's Settings. That key is encrypted on your device using AES-GCM with a non-extractable key held in the Android KeyStore, and the encrypted result is stored in the app's private preferences. The plaintext key is used only to talk to OpenRouter on your behalf. It is never written to the app's data files, your conversations, or logs.

What is sent to the AI service

When you interact with the built-in assistant, the following are sent to OpenRouter and the AI model provider you selected, so they can generate a response:

  • your prompts (what you type to the assistant),
  • the current app/world content the assistant is working on,
  • any photo you choose to attach.

This data is handled under the privacy policies of OpenRouter and the respective model provider. See OpenRouter's privacy policy. quine's developer does not receive or store this data.

Photos

A photo you select is processed on your device (decoded and resized) and its image bytes are sent as part of the AI request to OpenRouter. The conversation stored locally on your device keeps only the text; the image bytes are stripped from the stored conversation.

No analytics or tracking

quine uses no analytics and no third-party tracking SDKs. The only network communication the app makes is the request to OpenRouter described above.

Children

quine is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

As this is an experimental research app, this policy may change. The "Last updated" date above reflects the current version.
